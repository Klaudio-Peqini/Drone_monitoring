# Phase 1 — Baseline CFD model of the drone sensor enclosure

Baseline assumptions: OpenFOAM on Ubuntu; 150 × 150 × 150 mm enclosure; one 10 mm-ID inlet tube and one 10 mm-ID outlet tube, each 40 mm long; opposite centred openings; 2 L/min incompressible isothermal airflow; simplified PM, temperature–humidity, pressure-sensor and PCB obstacles. Phase 1 determines the principal flow path, recirculation, stagnant regions, short-circuiting, sensor-adjacent velocity, pressure drop and flow balance before any pollutant, particle, thermal, humidity, rotor or external-flow physics are introduced.

This file is a concise archival description. The full working Phase 1 package is retained in `reference_archives/drone_sensor_cfd_phase1.zip`.
